## Observation policy (hidden data)

Ingested as targets only if visible, interactable, and not fully clipped off the document origin with positive layout evidence.

**Excluded:** `display:none`, `visibility:hidden`, `hidden`, `aria-hidden=true`, `inert`, `opacity:0`, `pointer-events:none`, `input[type=hidden]`, fully offscreen-left/above when width/height > 0.

**Kept:** below-the-fold, accessibility names of **visible** controls (aria-label may contribute a label and therefore local risk — that is fail-safe HIGH, not policy takeover).

## Residual risks (not claimed closed)

- Keyword paraphrases that do **not** hit the consequential-label list and are **not** form-submit/file structure remain LOW. Universal web semantics are out of scope.
- `confirmationId` is in `ProductState` (overlay). Page world cannot send extension messages. A modified content script could echo the id; live revalidation still applies.
- `_isUserConfirmed` is a type brand, not cryptography.
- Formal leakage P/R/F1 and performance benches are later gates.
- Real Chrome unpacked E2E is MANUAL.
